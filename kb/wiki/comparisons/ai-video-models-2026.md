## The post-Sora landscape

With Sora's discontinuation, the market is now a three-horse race:

1. **Runway Gen-4.5** — the production workhorse. Best camera control, longest clips, strongest ecosystem. The only tool you can plan a client deliverable around.
2. **Google Veo 3.1** — the narrative and audio leader. Native audio is genuinely useful. Best pick for enterprise teams on GCP.
3. **Kling 3.0** — the value cinematic option. Only model with native 4K. Strong multi-language audio. Worth testing alongside Runway.

**Pika occupies a separate niche** — it's not competing at the high end. It's the social media machine: fast, cheap, fun, and good enough for short-form content. Most creators should use Pika alongside a higher-quality model, deploying each where it performs best.
