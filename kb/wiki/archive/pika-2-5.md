# Pika 2.5

The fastest and most affordable AI video generator. Pika prioritizes speed and creative expression over cinematic realism — ideal for social media content, viral effects, and high-volume experimentation.

## Capabilities

- **Fastest generation** — 8-15 seconds per clip
- **Pikaffects** — viral effects: melt, explode, crush, inflate
- **Pikaformance** — lip-sync for dialogue (3 credits/sec)
- **Pikascenes / Pikaframes** — up to 25s interpolation
- **SoundGen** — AI sound effects
- **Pikaswaps** — object/person replacement
- **Camera control** — pan, tilt, zoom
- **Free tier has no watermark**
