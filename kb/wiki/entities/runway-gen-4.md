# Runway Gen-4

The professional standard for AI video generation in 2026. Runway Gen-4 (and Gen-4.5) is the default recommendation for production workflows — the tool that behaves like part of a professional pipeline, not just a tech demo.

## Capabilities

- **Motion Brush** — paint motion onto specific regions of a frame
- **Camera Control** — pan, zoom, dolly, arc movements on demand
- **Director Mode** — multi-scene character consistency
- **Lip Sync** — match mouth movements to audio
- **Green Screen / Expand Video / Act-Two** motion capture
- **Aleph** — in-video editor for clip modification
- **Workflows** — custom composable pipelines
- **Gen-4.5** added native audio generation and editing
